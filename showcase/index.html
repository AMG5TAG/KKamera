The problem

Where do your job photos end up?

On most phones, a photo of a switchboard sits next to your kids' birthday. It syncs to your personal cloud, and it leaves with you when you change jobs or phones.

With the built-in camera
  • Work shots mixed into your personal camera roll
  • Backed up to your personal account, not the business
  • Sent to the office by text, email or not at all
  • Show someone a family photo and scroll past a client's bathroom
With KKamera
  • Uploads to the business cloud you choose, into a /KKamera folder you can rename
  • Send each shot to one account or to several at once
  • The phone copy is deleted after upload by default
  • Turn off Save to Photos and work shots never reach your camera roll
What you get

Your cloud, not ours

KKamera doesn't keep your photos. They go to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, or to a Synology NAS, Nextcloud, any WebDAV server or FTP.

How it works

Set it up once. Then just shoot.

  1. Connect your cloud

    Sign in to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, or enter your server's details. Pick the folder uploads land in.

  2. Take the photo

    Use KKamera instead of the built-in camera. Photo, video, panorama, document scan or interval shots.

  3. It's filed

    The shot uploads straight away, or queues until you have a connection. A badge on screen confirms where it went.
